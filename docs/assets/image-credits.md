# GemologyX image credits and licenses

## Sapphire profile specimen gallery

The following images are hosted locally under `public/images/gems/` for page performance and to avoid hotlinking. They depict rough sapphire (corundum) crystals from the Missouri River deposit in Montana, USA. Do not caption or imply that these are Sri Lankan specimens.

- `sapphire-crystals-montana-01.jpg` — Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History, catalog NMNH 177074. [Collection record](https://naturalhistory.si.edu/object/nmnhmineralsciences_14176990). [Image source](https://ids.si.edu/ids/deliveryService?id=ark%3A%2F65665%2Fm3f51d977c19984cfcbd92ad1a3198537e&max=1200). Licensed CC0 by Smithsonian Open Access.
- `sapphire-crystals-montana-02.jpg` — same collection record and license. [Image source](https://ids.si.edu/ids/deliveryService?id=ark%3A%2F65665%2Fm3d19f6b1ca0d944f1af8e4c14afe37d85&max=1200). Licensed CC0 by Smithsonian Open Access.

The Smithsonian record identifies the specimens as sapphire crystals from Montana and marks the online media CC0. The record does not show a photographer name, so the site credits the holding institution and collection record. The GemologyX caption clarifies the depicted locality and states the images do not prove the origin of any other stone.

For future media, verify the license on the specific asset page, keep the original source URL and license beside the local copy, and preserve required creator attribution. Smithsonian CC0 is one useful source; not every Smithsonian item is CC0.
