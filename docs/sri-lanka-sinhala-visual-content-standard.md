# Sri Lanka and Sinhala visual content standard

This standard applies to the English Sri Lanka hub, the Sinhala learning hub, all Sri Lanka industry guides, and future Sinhala articles and interactive learning components.

## Visual direction
- Use the GemologyX pearl-white, ruby and champagne editorial identity in light mode, with a considered dark-mode equivalent.
- Sinhala typography must use the bundled Noto Sans Sinhala font variable, generous line height, comfortable line length and no forced Latin-style letter spacing.
- Prefer real documentary photographs for mining, cutting, sorting, gem inspection and trade contexts.
- Use original diagrams for geology, process flows and instrument explanations where a photograph cannot explain the concept accurately.
- Never present a decorative illustration, generic workshop, unrelated mineral or AI-generated visual as documentary evidence.
- Do not add imagery merely to fill space. Each image must support the nearby subject.

## Image licensing and attribution
1. Open and verify the individual Wikimedia Commons file page before use.
2. Record the exact file-page URL, author/photographer, licence and attribution wording.
3. Display a visible credit and link to the Commons file page near the image.
4. Link to the licence where appropriate; preserve ShareAlike obligations when adapting CC BY-SA material.
5. Do not crop, recolour or otherwise modify a photograph in a way that misrepresents a gemstone, location, person or working practice.
6. Keep alt text factual. Do not imply that a specimen was mined at a named locality unless the source supports that claim.

## Requirements for future pages
- Every new major Sri Lanka or Sinhala guide must include either a relevant, licensed documentary photograph or a clearly labelled original explanatory diagram.
- Every new interactive explorer should have one meaningful visual anchor, not a row of decorative icons alone.
- Use the ArticleLayout optional heroImage prop for article photography. Include src, factual alt, visible credit, and sourceUrl.
- Use the shared gx-sri-photo-feature / gx-sri-photo-banner presentation classes for responsive, credited images.
- Do not reuse the same image across unrelated topics just to satisfy a visual quota.
- Keep source/credit information visible in both light and dark themes and on mobile.
