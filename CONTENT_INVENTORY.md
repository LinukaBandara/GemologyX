## 6. Working Rules

1. **No duplicate intent** — one primary keyword / question per page.
2. **Link or die** — every new page must link to at least 2–3 related pages.
3. **Trust first** — never state origin, treatment, or value as certain from appearance alone.
4. **Batch publish** — prefer 5–8 solid pages over 20 thin ones.
5. **Review dates** — every page gets a last-reviewed date; price-sensitive pages reviewed more often.
